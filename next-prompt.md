An editorial, magazine-style landing page for BAKALKOPI, an authentic Indonesian culinary and artisanal coffee sanctuary balancing unhurried tropical warmth with high-end editorial sophistication.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Platform:** Web, Desktop-first (Responsive 12-column editorial grid, container max-w-7xl)
- **Theme:** Authentic Indonesian Artisanal Hospitality — alternating between tactile unbleached cream daylight spreads and rich espresso charcoal nocturnal blocks connected by organic SVG wave dividers
- **Daylight Canvas Background:** Warm Parchment Cream (`#F3EEE5` / `#FDFBF7`)
- **Nocturnal Background:** Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#171512` / `#161616`)
- **Primary CTA & Buttons:** Deep Roasted Bean Brown (`#5A3827`) transitioning to Roasted Crema Gold (`#B98A4A`) on hover
- **Secondary / Highlight Accent:** Roasted Crema & Vintage Gold (`#B98A4A` / `#D99B26`) for badges, prices, starburst seals, and ambient lighting
- **Surface Elevation / Cards:** Pure Ceramic White (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px Earthen Border Taupe (`#E4DBD0`) and soft diffused umber drop shadows (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(31, 30, 29, 0.07)`)
- **Nocturnal Plates:** Charcoal Slate (`#1F1E1D`) with subtle hairline border and golden rim illumination
- **Typography:**
  - *Headlines & Displays:* Playfair Display (Serif) — Display Hero (64px, weight 600, tracking -0.02em), Headline Large (44px, weight 600), Headline Medium (32px, weight 500), Headline Small (22px, weight 600)
  - *Body, Microcopy & Navigation:* Plus Jakarta Sans (Geometric Sans-Serif) — Body Large (18px), Body Medium (15px), Body Small (13px), Price Tag (16px bold)
  - *Eyebrows & Metadata:* Plus Jakarta Sans Uppercase (11px bold, letter-spacing 0.14em - 0.25em)
- **Geometry & Shapes:**
  - Buttons: Full capsule pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`)
  - Cards & Imagery Containers: Generously rounded corners (`border-radius: 1rem` to `1.5rem` / 16px to 24px)
  - Floating Overlays: Frosted glassmorphic backdrop (`backdrop-blur-md` with 85% opacity)
